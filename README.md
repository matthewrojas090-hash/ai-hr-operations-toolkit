# AI HR Operations Toolkit

Practical, human-reviewed AI workflows for HR operations, employee experience, manager enablement, and workforce planning.

## Why this exists

HR teams spend significant time organizing policies, onboarding activities, manager guidance, workforce information, and employee-facing documentation. AI can help structure that work, but qualified people must verify the output, protect employee privacy, and remain accountable for decisions.

This portfolio project draws on Matthew Rojas's experience in recruiting leadership, onboarding, operational design, reporting, process documentation, and responsible AI adoption.

## Available resources

| Resource | HR need | Output |

| [AI Employee Onboarding Plan Builder](onboarding/employee-onboarding-plan-builder.md) | Onboarding information is scattered across teams and documents | Preboarding checklist, first-week plan, 30/60/90-day milestones, ownership map, risks, and employee-facing plan |

## Responsible use

These resources organize approved information. They should not make employment decisions, provide legal conclusions, diagnose performance, infer protected characteristics, or replace qualified HR review.

Remove personal, medical, compensation, accommodation, investigation, and employee-relations information before using an external AI system. Follow your organization's privacy, security, retention, and AI policies.
